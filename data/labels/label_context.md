**Reference:** 12 months. Only graduates who completed a master's (Level 9) can be given a further 12 months.

**Answer:** You can stay on Stamp 1G for 12 months, unless you completed a master’s degree programme, in which case you may be granted a further 12 months [1]. 

After the initial or extended period of 12 months, you must find a job that requires an employment permit and follow the usual application process with Department of Enterprise Trade and Employment [1].

## 2. Can I use the graduate programme again if I get another degree later

**Reference:** Yes, a graduate who achieves a higher-level award (e.g. Level 9 or above) can re-enter the programme for another 12 months, subject to the overall eight-year limit, but nobody can access the programme on more than two separate occasions.

**Answer:** Yes, you can re-enter the Third Level Graduate Programme for an additional period of 12 months if you achieve a higher level award, such as level 9 or above, after previously benefiting from the programme at a level 8 award or above [1]. However, this is subject to the overall limit of eight years and you must not have already exceeded two separate occasions of accessing the Programme [1].

## 8. Can I book my registration appointment before I arrive in Ireland

**Reference:** No. You book after you arrive: when you enter Ireland your passport gets a landing stamp instructing you to register within 90 days, and appointments should only be booked after that instruction.

**Answer:** No [2]. You should only book an appointment after you receive an instruction to register on arrival in the State, and your passport will be endorsed with a landing stamp instructing that you register within 90 days [3].
