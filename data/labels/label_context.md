## 1. When do I need to apply for Stamp 1G

**Reference:** Within six months of being notified by the awarding body or institution that you have achieved your award. Provisional results are not sufficient.

**Answer:** You must apply for a Stamp 1G immigration permission within six months of being notified by the relevant awarding body or institution that you have achieved the award for which you had enrolled as a student [1]. You can only qualify for a Stamp 1G permission after your provider has issued final results and you can evidence that you have achieved the award [1]. Provisional results will not suffice [1].

## 2. I just finished my masters can I stay in Ireland and look for a job

**Reference:** Yes, if eligible, under the Third Level Graduate Programme (Stamp 1G), which lets graduates stay after their studies to look for graduate-level employment, subject to conditions such as applying within six months of being notified of the award.

**Answer:** You may be eligible to remain in Ireland under the Third Level Graduate Programme, which allows you to stay for 12 months after completing your studies [1]. However, you must register with Immigration Service Delivery if you are from outside the EU, UK or Switzerland and have been in Ireland for more than 90 days [2].

## 3. How long is the graduate permission for a level 9 masters degree

**Reference:** 12 months initially, renewable for a further 12 months (24 months in total) if you show you have taken steps to find graduate-level employment, subject to the overall eight-year limit.

**Answer:** Graduates with an award at Level 9 or above on the National Framework of Qualifications who qualify for the Programme will be granted permission for 12 months initially [1]. This will be renewed for a further period of twelve months (subject to the overall eight year limit on student conditions not being exceeded) where the graduate satisfies the immigration authorities that he or she has taken appropriate steps to access suitable graduate level employment [1].

Note: The sources do not specify a specific duration for a Level 9 masters degree, but rather provide general information about the Programme.

## 5. What is Stamp 0

**Reference:** Permission to stay in Ireland for a temporary period, subject to conditions such as being financially self-sufficient (or fully supported by a sponsor), no access to benefits or publicly funded services, and having private medical insurance.

**Answer:** Stamp 0 indicates permission to stay in Ireland for a temporary period, subject to conditions [2]. You must be of independent means and fully financially self-sufficient, or your sponsor in Ireland must support you fully [2]. You cannot receive any benefits or use publicly funded services, and you must have private medical insurance [2].

## 8. What documents do I need to bring to my first registration appointment

**Reference:** It depends on your stamp. All applicants need certain documents (e.g. passport, proof of address, the relevant visa/landing stamp, medical insurance where required) plus documents specific to their stamp category. A good answer explains this or asks which stamp the person has.

**Answer:** For a Stamp 2A Registration, you will need to bring:
- The biometric page of your current valid passport(s) [5]
- A letter from your college/school confirming your enrolment as a student [5]
- Proof that you have paid college/school fees [5]
- Private Medical Insurance or Travel Insurance with medical cover for accidents and medical incident including hospital stays in Ireland, valid for 12 months, or if less than 12 months – for the duration of your stay [3, 5]
- Proof of your exam results [5]

For a Stamp 1G Registration, you will need to bring:
- The biometric page of you and your spouse’s/ de facto’s current valid passports [3]
- The relevant permit of your spouse / de facto partner [3]
- Private Medical Insurance or Travel Insurance with medical cover for accidents and medical incident including hospital stays in Ireland, valid for 12 months, or if less than 12 months – for the duration of your stay [3]

For a Stamp 2 Registration, you will need to bring:
- A Mobility Agreement, Learning Agreement, or Inter-institutional Agreement [4]
